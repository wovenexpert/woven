WOVEN EXPERT WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html
- script.js
- assets/logo-dark.png (your dark logo)
- assets/logo-white.png (your white logo)

Run: Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a browser, or use VS Code Live Server.
Internet is required for Bootstrap, icons, Google Fonts, remote imagery and the hero background video.

The hero video is a remote Pexels MP4 and is muted, looping and inline. If it cannot load, its poster image is shown.

The contact forms validate fields and open the visitor's email app with a prefilled message to wovenexpert@gmail.com. They do not send email automatically.
Replace the social media # links with your real profile URLs before publishing.
